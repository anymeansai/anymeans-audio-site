How it works

Every preset is a whole vocal chain.

There are 59 chains for lead vocals and 37 for ad-libs, sorted into ten genres: Pop, R&B, Trap, Rage, Hip-Hop, Soul, Afrobeats, Indie, Hyperpop and Plugg.

Each one is a complete chain of effects, and you can open it and change anything inside. Or start from an empty chain and build your own.

01

Pick a preset

It opens on Pop - Radio Ready. Browse the rest by genre, or press New Chain and start from nothing.

02

Set the key

Choose it in the header, detect it from the vocal, or put the free Vocalyst Auto Key on your beat and let it send the key over.

03

Press Analyze

Play the vocal and Vocalyst suggests gate, de-esser, compression and EQ settings for that take. One click applies them.

Analyze

Start from settings that fit your take.

Press Analyze and play the song. Vocalyst measures the raw vocal while it plays: the noise floor, how loud the loud and quiet phrases are, plosives, sibilance, the overall tilt and any resonances that stick out.

Then it proposes a low cut, gate, de-esser, compressor and EQ, with the reason for each one. Apply them all, or read the reasons and set it up yourself.

Tune

Pitch correction is built in.

Set the key and scale, click notes on the keyboard to allow or block them, and pick how hard it pulls. Natural keeps slides and vibrato. Tight is quick but still human. Hard is the instant snap.

There are two engines. Modern keeps the singer's formants where they are, so big corrections still sound like the same person. Classic moves them with the pitch, for the older hard-tuned sound.

Recording? Press Live and Tune switches to a mode with almost no delay, so the singer can track through the whole chain with correction on.

Simple mode

Six knobs, when that's all you need.

Simple mode puts six macros on top of whatever preset is loaded. They bend the preset without rewriting it. With every knob in the middle, you hear the preset exactly as it was designed.

Want the detail? Switch to Advanced and every parameter of every effect is there.

Tune
How strongly and how quickly the pitch gets corrected.
Clean
Gate, de-esser and compression, together.
Tone

Advanced mode

29 effects, in any order you like.

Advanced mode lays the chain out as a row of tiles. Click one to edit it, drag it to move it, add any effect from the list or take one out. Every effect has its own preset menu too, from gentle de-essing to a dotted throw on the delay.

Questions

Which DAWs does it work in?

Anything that loads VST3, like Ableton Live, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Bitwig and Reaper. On a Mac it's also an Audio Unit, so it works in Logic Pro and GarageBand too.

Will it work in Pro Tools?

No. Pro Tools only loads AAX, and Vocalyst doesn't come in AAX.

Can I record through it?

Yes. Press Live in the footer and Tune switches to a mode with almost no delay. Every other effect already runs without latency, so the singer can track through the whole chain with tuning on. Turn Live off when you mix and Tune goes back to its studio engine.

Which effects are in it?

Seven for cleanup (gate, pop filter, breath control, low cut, high cut, de-esser and Tune), four for dynamics (compressor, glue compressor, limiter and transient shaper), two for tone (a four-band EQ and air), five for color (saturation, distortion, exciter, lo-fi and telephone), eight for modulation (doubler, harmonizer, chorus, flanger, phaser, tremolo, auto-pan and widener) and three for space (slap, a tempo-synced delay and reverb).

What's Vocalyst Auto Key?

A small free plug-in that goes on the beat or the master bus and works out the song's key. Press Auto Key in Vocalyst to pull it in, or link the two and the key follows on its own.

Is there a version that only does the tuning?

Yes. Vocalyst Tune is the Tune page as a plug-in of its own, with the same engine. It comes in the same download and works with your Vocalyst key.

I can hear a dry vocal under the processed one. Why?

Vocalyst is fully wet by default, so the dry voice is coming from somewhere else. Check that direct monitoring on your audio interface is off, and that there isn't a second copy of the vocal track playing. If you want some dry signal on purpose, that's what the Dry/Wet knob in the footer is for.

Can I share my presets?

Yes. Press Share and your chain is saved as a .vocalystpreset file in the Community folder. Send the file to someone, they add it to theirs, and it shows up in their Community tab. No account needed.

How do I get my key?

It's emailed to you right after checkout, along with the download links. Activate it once and Vocalyst works offline from then on.

Can I move it to a new computer?

Yes. Deactivate the key in Settings on the old computer, then activate it on the new one. If the old computer is gone, email support@anymeansaudio.com.
