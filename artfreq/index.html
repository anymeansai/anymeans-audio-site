ArtFreq

VST3 macOS Windows

ArtFreq lets you draw in sound. Type, paint, or drop in a picture and it plays back in time with your track, showing up in the spectrogram.

Choose your version

Draw something on the canvas and ArtFreq plays it as sound, in time with your track.

Three ways to draw

  • Text: type words on the canvas, they play back as sound
  • Image: drop in a picture or a logo
  • Brush: paint freehand on the canvas

Features

  • In time: loops with your track, 1 beat to 32 bars
  • Canvas: 20 Hz to 20 kHz, log scale
  • Layers: stack drawings on top of each other
  • Bands: split the canvas into frequency bands
  • Spectrogram: your drawing shows up in the sound
  • Bounce: export your drawing as a WAV
Pro includes both plug-ins: ArtFreq and ArtFreq FX. One purchase, one key, both in the same download.

ArtFreq FX is the effect version. Run your own sound through a drawing. It also includes everything in Standard.

FX modes

  • Carve: your sound passes through the drawing. What's drawn stays, the rest is cut
  • Carve + Draw: your sound and the drawing at the same time
  • Stencil: your sound takes the drawing's shape. The gaps go quiet
  • Draw: the drawing plays on its own, like the instrument

FX features

  • Depth: how far the un-drawn parts are cut, −120 to 0 dB, −80 by default
  • Same canvas: text, image and brush tools, same as the instrument
  • Latency: 85.3 ms, reported to your DAW

Requirements

  • VST3 plug-in
  • macOS and Windows

Works in any DAW that hosts VST3 plug-ins.

Questions

How do I get my key?

It arrives by email right after checkout, with the download links.

Can I move it to a new computer?

Yes. Deactivate the key inside the plug-in on the old machine, then activate it on the new one.
