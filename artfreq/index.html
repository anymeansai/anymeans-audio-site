ArtFreq

VST3 macOS Windows

ArtFreq lets you draw in sound. Type, paint, or drop in a picture and it plays back in time with your track, showing up in the spectrogram.

Draw it your way

Type a word, drop in a logo, paint a sweep. If it shows on the canvas, it plays.

In time with the song

One beat or thirty-two bars. ArtFreq loops your drawing in time with the track.

Details

  • 20 Hz to 20 kHz canvas, log scale
  • Length from 1 beat to 32 bars
  • Layers for stacking drawings
  • Frequency bands
  • Bounce to WAV

ArtFreq FX

ArtFreq FX is the effect version of ArtFreq. It comes with ArtFreq Pro.

  • Modes: Carve, Carve + Draw, Stencil, Draw
  • Depth: -120 to 0 dB, default -80 dB
  • Latency: 85.3 ms

Versions

Standard is ArtFreq on its own, $29. Pro is ArtFreq plus ArtFreq FX, $39.

Requirements

  • VST3 plug-in
  • macOS and Windows

Works in any DAW that hosts VST3 plug-ins.

Licensing

Questions

Which DAWs does it work in?

Any DAW that hosts VST3 plug-ins.

Mac and Windows?

Both. ArtFreq is VST3 for macOS and Windows.

What does Pro add?

ArtFreq FX, the effect version. Everything in Standard is included.

How do I get my key?

It arrives by email right after checkout.

Can I move it to a new computer?

Yes. Deactivate the key inside the plug-in on the old machine, then activate it on the new one.
