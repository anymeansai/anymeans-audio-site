Crème

Brighter highs that don't get harsh

Mastering engineers have a trick for this. They send the track to a spare bus, cut everything but the highs, crush those with a very fast limiter and blend them back in under the original. Crème does all of that inside one plug-in, with no bus to set up and no latency.

Any track or the master VST3 · macOS · Windows
