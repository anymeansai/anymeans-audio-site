ArtFreq

Put words and pictures in your spectrogram

Type some text or drop in an image and press play. ArtFreq turns it into sound, and that sound draws your picture on any spectrogram analyzer, in time with your project.

VST3 · macOS · Windows

The canvas is the analyzer screen

Time runs left to right, anywhere from 1 beat to 32 bars, and it follows your project's tempo, so a 4-bar drawing lasts exactly 4 bars. Frequency runs bottom to top, 20 Hz to 20 kHz on a log scale by default.

01

Load it on an instrument track

ArtFreq makes its own sound, so it goes on a MIDI or instrument track.

02

Open a spectrogram

Put any analyzer with a scrolling spectrogram on the output.

03

Match the settings

Set Scale, Low, High and Slope in ArtFreq's settings to whatever your analyzer uses.

04

Press play

It plays once from wherever you start playback. You can also loop it, start it at a set bar, or trigger it with MIDI notes.

A spectrogram built in

Flip the switch at the top of the window from Canvas to Live and ArtFreq shows its own output as a scrolling spectrogram, on the same scale as the canvas. It's handy for a quick check when you don't have an analyzer open.

Layers and frequency bands

Every text box and image gets its own layer. You can hide or reorder them in the Layers panel and snap any of them into the low, mid or high band. Text comes in five fonts, solid or outlined.

Small text reads best up high. Analyzers have much more detail in the top octaves, and the bottom few always come out blurry.

Bounce to WAV

Bounce WAV renders one pass of the drawing in the background, and you drag the file straight into your arrangement. If you change the drawing afterward, it renders a new file by itself, so you never end up dragging in an old version.

Carve the drawing into your own audio

ArtFreq FX comes with ArtFreq Pro. It goes on audio you already have, like a vocal, a beat or your whole mix. It cuts the drawing's frequencies out of that audio, so the drawing shows up on the spectrogram inside your track.

Carve

Mutes the frequencies under the drawing, so it shows up as dark shapes in the song.

Carve + Draw

Carves a slightly bigger hole and plays the drawing's own sound inside it, so you get bright shapes with a dark outline.

Stencil

The reverse of Carve. Only the audio inside the drawing gets through and the rest is muted.

There's also a Draw mode that plays the drawing on top without cutting anything.

Questions

Which DAWs does it work in?

Any 64-bit DAW that loads VST3 plug-ins, on macOS or Windows.

Will it work in Logic or Pro Tools?

No. Logic only loads Audio Units and Pro Tools only loads AAX. ArtFreq is VST3.

Which analyzer should I use?

Any analyzer with a scrolling spectrogram. If it shows about 8 seconds across the screen, a drawing about 8 seconds long fits in one piece.

Does it need an internet connection?

Only to activate or deactivate. After that it checks the license on your computer and doesn't go online.

Can I move it to a new computer?

Yes. Deactivate the key inside the plug-in on the old machine, then activate it on the new one. If the old machine is gone, email support@anymeansaudio.com.
