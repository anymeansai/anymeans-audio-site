How it works

The canvas is a picture of your spectrogram.

Left to right is time, from one beat up to 32 bars, locked to your DAW's tempo. Bottom to top is frequency, 20 Hz to 20 kHz on a log scale, the way most analysers draw it.

So whatever you put on the canvas is what you'll see on the analyser.

01

Draw something

Add text, drop in an image or paint with the brush. PNG, JPEG and GIF work on both systems, and on a Mac so do HEIC, WebP and TIFF.

02

Press play

It plays once from wherever you hit play. You can also loop it every few bars, pin it to a bar of the song, or fire it from MIDI notes.

03

Open an analyser

Put MiniMeters, or any scrolling spectrogram, on the output and watch your drawing go by.

Live view

Check it without leaving the plug-in.

Switch from Canvas to Live and ArtFreq shows its own output as a scrolling spectrogram, on the same scale as the canvas. It's the quickest way to see whether a drawing reads the way you meant it to.

Layers

Stack text, pictures and paint.

Every text box and picture gets its own layer. Reorder them, hide one, or snap it into the low, mid or high band. Text comes in five fonts, solid or outline.

Small detail reads best up high. Analysers have the most resolution there, so that's where text looks sharpest.

Export

Bounce it to a WAV.

Bounce WAV renders one pass in the background and hands you a file to drag straight into your arrangement. Change the drawing, the length or the level and it renders again, so the file always matches the canvas.

Density
16 to 1024 lines. Low for a scan-line look, high for solid shapes.
Contrast
How far the faint parts sit under the bright ones, 12 to 72 dB.
Lows
Smooth keeps low shapes filled in. Stripes plays separate lines instead.
Level
Output volume. The loudest moment sits at −12 dBFS RMS before this.

ArtFreq FX · Comes with Pro

Carve the drawing into a song.

The instrument makes its own sound. ArtFreq FX works on audio you already have. Put it on a vocal, a bus or the master and the drawing shows up inside the mix, even when the track is dense.

Carve

Mutes the drawing's frequencies, so it shows up as dark shapes in the song.

Carve + Draw

Cuts a slightly wider hole and plays the drawing inside it. Bright shapes with a dark outline.

Stencil

The other way round. Only the drawing lets the song through and everything else goes quiet.

There's a fourth mode, Draw, which plays the drawing on top of the song and leaves the audio alone.

Questions

Which DAWs does it work in?

Any DAW that loads VST3 plug-ins. That covers Ableton Live, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Bitwig and Reaper, among others.

Will it work in Logic or Pro Tools?

No. Logic only loads Audio Units and Pro Tools only loads AAX. ArtFreq is VST3.

Which analyser should I use?

Anything with a scrolling spectrogram. MiniMeters works well. ArtFreq's defaults (log scale, 20 Hz to 20 kHz) match a standard MiniMeters setup, and if yours is set up differently you can match it in ArtFreq's settings.

What does Pro add?

ArtFreq FX, the effect version, which carves the drawing into audio you already have. Pro gets you both plug-ins on one key, in the same download.

Do I need to send it MIDI?

No. Out of the box it plays when you press play in your DAW. MIDI triggering is there if you'd rather each note fire the drawing.

How do I get my key?

It's emailed to you right after checkout, along with the download links.

Can I move it to a new computer?

Yes. Deactivate the key inside the plug-in on the old machine, then activate it on the new one. If the old machine is gone, email support@anymeansaudio.com.
